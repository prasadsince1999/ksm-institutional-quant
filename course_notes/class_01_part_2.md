## 2. VISUAL CANDLESTICK & STRUCTURE RULES

### A. Major vs. Minor / Inside Candles
- **Major Candle Creation & Transfer [00:20 - 00:40, 01:30 - 01:50]**:
  - A candle is established as a Major Candle when it sets a new high (in an uptrend) or low (in a downtrend).
  - If the next candle gaps or extends a wick above the current major candle's high, the designation of "Major Candle" instantly transfers to that newest candle.
- **Ignoring Inside / Minor Candles**:
  - If subsequent candles do not sweep the low of the major candle and do not break its high, they are internal inside candles. Price action moving within that range is completely ignored—no pullback is validated.

### B. Valid Pullback / Sweep (Wick-to-Body & Mitigation Criteria)
- **Valid Pullback Mechanics [00:25 - 00:40, 01:40 - 01:55]**:
  - In an uptrend, a pullback is valid only when price sweeps or breaks the low of the major candle (by wick or body) and is followed by a continuation candle in trend direction.
  - If no candle breaches the major candle's low, price has moved straight up with no valid pullback.
- **Strict IDM Mitigation Sweep Rule [02:45 - 03:02]**:
  - **Equal Lows DO NOT Mitigate IDM**: If a candle wick merely touches the IDM line or forms an exact equal low (*"For suppose ek wick equal low create kar de... isko hum nahi bolenge inducement mitigation hua hai"*), it is invalid.
  - **The 1-Point Penetration Requirement**: To confirm inducement mitigation, price must physically pierce at least 1 point/tick beyond the IDM level (*"iske niche at least 1 point also iske niche aana padega"*).
- **Breakout vs. Sweep (The Shifting Level Rule) [03:40 - 04:05, 06:40 - 07:00, 07:40 - 07:55, 11:55 - 12:05]**:
  - **Valid Breakout**: Requires a full candle body close beyond the structural level (HH, LL, BOS, or CHOCH).
  - **Fake Breakout / Sweep**: If price only pierces the line with a wick and closes back inside, it is merely a liquidity sweep.
  - **Mechanical Action**: The level is not confirmed; the horizontal line must be shifted to the extreme tip of that wick.

### C. Swing High / Swing Low Lookback Logic
- **No Arbitrary Candle Count Lookback**: The mentor does not count a set number of bars (e.g., 3-candle or 5-candle fractals).
- **Conditional Structural Anchors**:
  - **Uptrend Major Swing High (BOS line)**: Confirmed only after price mitigates the IDM. It is placed at the highest candle wick/body peak between the initial breakout and the IDM mitigation point [03:05 - 03:20].
  - **Uptrend Major Swing Low (CHOCH line)**: Placed at the lowest swing low point below IDM that originated the leg. It is locked in once the BOS line is broken with a full candle body [04:00 - 04:15, 07:05 - 07:20].
  - **Downtrend Major Swing Low (BOS line)**: Placed at the lowest point of the impulsive leg once IDM is mitigated [09:40 - 10:05, 12:15 - 12:25].
  - **Downtrend Major Swing High (CHOCH line)**: Placed at the highest peak above IDM that initiated the downward impulse [10:10 - 10:30, 11:10 - 11:25].

## 4. "DO NOT TRADE" / AVOIDANCE RULES

1. **Do Not Count Equal Lows as IDM Mitigation [02:45 - 03:05]**:
   - Avoid assuming inducement has been taken if price only matches the low. If price does not pierce the line by at least 1 point, do not mark BOS or assume the swing high is locked.
2. **Do Not Mark New IDM Without a Body Breakout [04:30 - 05:05, 05:40 - 06:00]**:
   - When price creates minor pullbacks while grinding upward, avoid marking a new IDM unless the previous swing high was broken by a candle body. As long as breaks occur by wicks only, the old IDM remains active.
3. **Do Not Over-Map Distant History in Live Trading [05:15 - 05:35, 14:15 - 14:40]**:
   - Skip drawing structural swings across 6–8 hours of historical data during live market sessions. In real trading, only map the most recent swing leg from where the market made its latest major U-turn.
4. **The "Bullish Momentum" Trap in a Confirmed Downtrend [10:35 - 11:05, 12:40 - 13:05]**:
   - *The Trap*: A sharp sequence of large green candles pushing higher with higher highs. Retail price action traders assume a new uptrend has begun and buy aggressively.
   - *The SMC Avoidance Rule*: Skip all buying! The market remains mechanically in a downtrend until price breaks and closes above the confirmed CHOCH line with a candle body. The rally is merely an internal retracement to collect liquidity or fill an FVG before collapsing back into a BOS.
