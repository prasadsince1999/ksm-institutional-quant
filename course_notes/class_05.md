## 2. VISUAL CANDLESTICK & STRUCTURE RULES

### A. Major vs. Minor / Inside Candles

- **Inside Candle Invalidation Rule**: Any candle whose entire high and low are contained within the high and low range of the preceding "mother candle" is an inside/minor candle. It has no structural weight.
- **Pullback Mapping**: When identifying an Inducement (IDM) or a pullback, you ignore price action occurring entirely within an inside candle. Only a candle that breaks the high/low of the mother candle creates a valid structural swing leg [01:56, 03:15].
- **Single-Candle Break Limitation**: If a high/low is broken by just one single candle without a distinct multi-candle pullback wave, it can technically serve as a minor inducement, but the mentor explicitly classifies it as a "weak level" that market will easily blow through to reach unmitigated extreme zones [03:20–03:45].

### B. Valid Pullback vs. Sweep (Wick-to-Body Relationship)

- **Valid Sweep (Liquidity Grab / Trap)**:
  - *Visual criterion*: Price pierces beyond a previous swing high or swing low strictly with its wick (shadow), and the candle body closes back inside the prior structural level [20:45, 24:45, 28:15–28:40].
  - *Meaning*: Liquidity/stop losses have been consumed, but no institutional commitment exists to break the level. This signals an impending sharp reversal.
- **Valid Breakout / Structural Break (BOS / CHoCH)**:
  - *Visual criterion*: The candle must close with a full candle body beyond the swing high or swing low. A wick-only breach is classified as a sweep/fake breakout, not a true structural transfer.
- **Order Flow Breakdown Criterion**:
  - If a candle body closes completely through an Order Flow zone, the Order Flow is broken/invalidated, not merely mitigated [24:45–25:05].

### C. Swing High / Swing Low Rules

- **Fractal Look-Back**: A swing high/low requires a swing point flanked by lower highs (for swing high) or higher lows (for swing low). Prasad's structure diagrams show a standard 3-to-5 candle structure [01:56–02:56].
- **Wave Requirement**: For a swing point to be considered strong and hold institutional orders, it must consist of a complete corrective leg (multi-candle wave) rather than minor single-candle retracements [19:00–19:30, 38:10].

## 4. "DO NOT TRADE" / AVOIDANCE RULES

1. **Avoid Trading Reversals at Fully Mitigated Zones [04:15–04:35, 21:00–21:30, 25:15–26:35]**:
   - *Visual Cue*: When every gap, imbalance, and order block within a prior leg has already been filled/mitigated.
   - *Reason*: The market has no institutional interest or liquidity left there. Price will break the level to target the next unmitigated POI or sweep the swing low.
2. **Avoid "Weak" Order Blocks [04:45–05:00]**:
   - *Visual Cue*: An Order Block that did not sweep a previous swing high or swing low.
   - *Reason*: Without a prior liquidity sweep, the order block is weak and has a low probability of holding.
3. **Avoid Counter-Trend / Continuation when Gap Creation Fails (3–5 Candle Rule) [10:45–11:10, 15:35–16:10, 17:30–17:45]**:
   - *Visual Cue*: Price mitigates a level, but after 3 to 5 candles, it fails to produce a fresh Fair Value Gap in the direction of the trade; instead, small, indecisive bodies appear.
   - *Reason*: This signals a lack of stamina/power. Expect an immediate trend failure and opposite expansion.
4. **Avoid Trading Against an Inverse Gap Flip [12:20–13:00, 16:20–16:45]**:
   - *Visual Cue*: Price breaches a bullish/bearish gap without reacting, followed immediately by the creation of an opposite gap.
   - *Reason*: Practical order flow has flipped. Abandon previous directional bias immediately.
5. **Avoid Obvious Support / Resistance Lines (Retail Trap Rule) [26:40–27:05]**:
   - *Visual Cue*: Price coming to rest right at an obvious horizontal support or resistance line where retail buyers enter.
   - *Reason*: Market makers intentionally engineer stop loss liquidity below/above these levels (*"mama ko trap karne ke baad"*) and will sweep them before true directional moves.
6. **Avoid Breakout Buys after Multiple Sweeps (Triple Sweep Trap) [31:10–31:30]**:
   - *Visual Cue*: Price attempts to break a high 3 or more times, producing wicks each time without body closes.
   - *Reason*: Buyers lack the volume to expand higher. Reversal to the downside is imminent.
7. **Skip / Do Not Trade during Dual Gap Invalidation (Consolidation Trap) [36:30–36:55]**:
   - *Visual Cue*: Price breaks a gap downward without printing a new bearish gap, then immediately breaks upward without printing a new bullish gap.
   - *Reason*: This indicates non-directional consolidation and manipulation. Skip the trade until clear imbalance expansion occurs.

## 5. TIMESTAMPED RULE SUMMARY

- **[03:15] Single-Candle Inducement Classification**: A single-candle break can be marked as IDM, but it is mechanically treated as a "weak level" that frequently fails.
- **[04:20] Rule of Unmitigated Liquidity**: Market moves from one unmitigated level to the next. Fully mitigated areas offer zero resistance/support.
- **[04:45] Order Block Strength Validation**: An Order Block is only valid and strong if it previously swept a prior swing high/low; otherwise, it is weak.
- **[05:45] Hierarchy Axiom (OF > OB)**: Order Flow takes precedence over Order Blocks because an Order Block is merely a sub-component within the larger Order Flow.
- **[06:20] Order Flow Box Definition**: In an uptrend, Order Flow is mechanically mapped as the entire corrective pullback leg located below the first Inducement (IDM).
- **[07:20] Order Flow Scenario 1 (Direct Tap & Expansion)**: If an Order Flow is completely unmitigated, price will reverse directly upon tapping its boundary or internal gap.
- **[07:50] Order Flow Scenario 2 (Deep Retracement)**: If the top of the Order Flow has already been mitigated, price will penetrate deeper to tap the unmitigated OB or extreme gap.
- **[08:15] Order Flow Scenario 3 (Sweep & Invalidation)**: If all internal levels of the Order Flow are mitigated, price will pierce through and sweep the structural swing low to grab stop losses.
- **[09:15] Technical vs. Practical Trend Divergence**: A market can remain in an uptrend technically (higher highs), but practical order flow can turn bearish immediately when gaps fail to hold.
- **[09:45] Practical Uptrend Mechanics**: Price repeatedly sweeps minor swing lows while respecting bullish gaps; every mitigation must lead to a new bullish gap.
- **[10:50] The 3-to-5 Candle Momentum / Stamina Filter**: After a gap mitigation, the dominant side MUST create a new gap within 3 to 5 candles. Failure to print a gap within 5 candles proves loss of institutional power.
- **[12:15] Practical Downtrend Mechanics**: Price sweeps swing highs and respects bearish gaps; failing to break previous bearish gaps confirms continued downside flow.
- **[13:30] Reversal Trigger (Gap Shift)**: A valid U-turn entry occurs when price fails to respect a prevailing trend gap, gives an indecisive reaction, and immediately generates an opposing gap.
- **[16:25] Inverse Fair Value Gap (IFVG) Principle**: When a gap is completely blown through with strong momentum, it inverts and acts as an opposite barrier upon retest.
- **[17:55] Execution on Gap Retest**: Do not trade until the market pulls back into the gap (*"retest kar diya market niche aa raha hai"*); enter on the mitigation.
- **[20:15] Low Sweep Mechanism**: An already-mitigated Order Flow forces the market to hunt the low of the original mother leg before expanding.
- **[20:45] Wick Sweep vs. Body Breakout Rule**: Wick penetrations equal sweeps (liquidity capture); candle body closures beyond levels equal genuine breaks.
- **[24:45] Order Flow Invalidation Rule**: If a full candle body closes below an Order Flow base, that Order Flow is destroyed, not mitigated.
- **[26:40] Support/Resistance Trap Rule**: Never enter long/short solely on support/resistance touch; wait for the liquidity sweep below support to avoid retail traps.
- **[28:15] Asset Universality Rule**: Practical order flow and gap respect/disrespect rules apply identically across Forex, Crypto (BTC), Equities (NIFTY), and Commodities (Gold).
- **[31:15] Triple Sweep Exhaustion Rule**: If a structural extreme is swept 3 consecutive times without a body close, market participants lack expansion energy; expect a sharp move toward Inducement.
- **[33:00] Stop Loss Gravitation Principle**: Market algorithmic order delivery targets liquidity pools (clusters of retail stop losses) located outside obvious swing levels and unmitigated imbalance pockets.
- **[36:30] Consolidation Identification & Avoidance**: Lack of gap generation in both directions after structural breaks indicates a trap/consolidation zone; trading must be paused.
- **[37:50] Gap Integrity Check**: An imbalance is strictly invalid if the wicks of candle 1 and candle 3 overlap. Genuine gaps must show clear empty space between candle 1 and candle 3.
- **[38:10] Structural Target Selection**: Target weak highs/lows (levels that never swept liquidity) as primary take-profit objectives, and expect reversals at strong highs/lows (levels that swept previous highs/lows).
