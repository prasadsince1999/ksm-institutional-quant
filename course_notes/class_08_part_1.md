## 1. TOPIC & CONTEXT

- **Core Concepts Taught**:
  - **Sharp Turn (ST)**: A high-probability institutional reversal pattern formed when price drives into a level with a "manipulation gap" and immediately rejects with an opposing "confirmation gap" (comparable to an institutional Balanced Price Range / BPR, used strictly as reversal confirmation).
  - **The 4-Step Mechanical Setup Checklist**:
    1. **Trend / Market Structure Identification**: Identifying major trend direction and active structural range.
    2. **Liquidity Point Identification**: Locating Inducement (IDM), Order Block (OB), Swing Highs/Lows, or Extreme FVG (E.FVG).
    3. **Reversal Confirmation**: Detecting a Sharp Turn (ST) or valid Gap.
    4. **Mandatory Breakout of Left-Side Manipulation Gap**: Ensuring the confirmation gap breaks past the first opposing left-side gap.
  - **Entry Checklist by Gap Type**:
    - **BAG (Breakaway Gap)**: 3rd candle inspection (Direct open entry vs. Retracement entry vs. BAG + CFG combination).
    - **FVG (Fair Value Gap)**: Mandatory mitigation (touch) requirement.
    - **CFG / C.FVG (Continuation Fair Value Gap)**: Direct entry rules when mitigating prior liquidity/gaps.
    - **Post-Mitigation Execution Rule**: Mandatory wick sweep of the previous candle's extreme (low in uptrends, high in downtrends).
    - **Target Definition**: Recent minor swing high/low vs. major target.
  - **Market Structure & Candlestick Mechanics**: Identification of genuine inducement vs. inside bars (sub-structure within a major candle range); body close vs. wick sweep.

## 2. VISUAL CANDLESTICK & STRUCTURE RULES

### A. Major vs. Minor / Inside Candles (Inside Bar Rule)

- **Inside Bar Containment Rule [30:08–30:25, 32:00–32:15]**:
  - Any candle whose entire high-to-low range is contained within the high and low of a preceding larger candle ("major mother candle") is treated as an inside/minor candle.
  - All internal fluctuations within the major candle’s body and wicks are **completely ignored** for swing structure, inducement, and pullbacks.
  - **Inside Candle Gap Trap**: Gaps created entirely within an inside candle’s territory are considered structural traps and cannot hold support or resistance.

### B. Valid Pullback vs. Sweep (Liquidity Grab)

- **Body Close vs. Wick Sweep [30:45–30:55]**:
  - **Valid Break of Structure (BOS)**: A candle must definitively close its **full body** beyond the key high/low level.
  - **Liquidity Sweep (Fake Breakout)**: If a candle pierces the high/low only with a wick and closes back inside the prior range, it is an institutional sweep, signaling an immediate reversal in the opposite direction.
- **Gap Penetration Wick Rule [32:15–32:30]**:
  - When evaluating a gap, if a candle enters the gap with a large, elongated piercing wick (*"bada wick"*), the market is seeking to fill or sweep past the gap rather than respect it.
  - For a gap to confirm holding, a candle body must cleanly close at or inside the boundary without an outsized piercing wick.
- **Post-Mitigation Sweep Prerequisite [27:00–27:20, 39:45–39:56]**:
  - After a level or gap is mitigated, an entry candle is **only valid if it visually sweeps the previous candle's low (for buy trades) or previous candle's high (for sell trades) before rejecting**.
  - If price mitigates a zone but turns without harvesting that previous candle extreme wick, liquidity remains uncollected, leaving the position vulnerable to a secondary flush.

### C. Swing High / Swing Low Lookback

- **Impulse Leg Lookback [10–15 Candles on 1m]**:
  - To identify a valid swing point or inducement (IDM), trace backwards candle-by-candle through impulsive legs (typically **10 to 15 candles** on the 1m chart) until finding the first previous candle whose low was cleanly taken out by a prior real candle body/range, actively bypassing all inside bars.

## 3. ENTRY & EXECUTION HABITS

### A. Order Timing & Safety Margin

- **Direct Entry (00s Open)**:
  - Allowed only when the 3rd candle of a BAG or CFG is a solid, decisive candle with a normal body and minimal opposing rejection wicks.
- **Retracement / Safety Margin**:
  - If the 3rd candle has an abnormal body, long upper/lower shadow, or is reacting to an opposing resistance/support area, **never click directly on candle open (00s)**.
  - The trader must wait for price to retrace into the mitigation zone/gap during the first 1–2 seconds (and strictly within the first 29–30 seconds) of the candle to secure a safety margin fill.

### B. Expiration Time

- **Strictly 1-Minute Expiration**:
  - Trades expire on the closing tick of the active 1-minute candle (fixed candle close: e.g., 00:01, 00:02 on Quotex timer panels).

## 4. "DO NOT TRADE" / AVOIDANCE RULES

1. **Inside-the-Manipulation-Gap Failure [14:20–15:30]**:
   - *Trigger*: When the reversal gap fails to breakout beyond the opposing left-side gap, closing inside its range.
   - *Reason*: The old manipulation gap remains active as resistance/support; sellers/buyers will resume the prior trend.
2. **Sharp Turn Without Preceding Liquidity [17:45–18:05, 33:40]**:
   - *Trigger*: A Sharp Turn or gap sequence that occurs in the middle of nowhere without first sweeping an external swing high/low or tapping an inducement level.
   - *Reason*: Pure institutional manipulation trap (*"fake momentum"*).
3. **Abnormal 3rd Candle Rejection Wick [24:45–25:05, 33:30–33:45]**:
   - *Trigger*: The 3rd confirmation candle shows a long wick opposing the trade direction or has an abnormal, exhausting spread.
   - *Reason*: Indicates counter-pressure; direct entry at candle open is prohibited.
4. **Inside Bar Structural Gaps [32:00–32:15]**:
   - *Trigger*: Gaps formed within the bounds of a single major candle.
   - *Reason*: Does not represent real institutional order flow; high failure rate.
5. **Premature Internal Confirmations [36:10–36:55]**:
   - *Trigger*: Reversal signals (e.g., lower-timeframe sweeps) printing before price reaches and mitigates the primary external target zone (Extreme FVG or Order Block).
   - *Reason*: Price will continue running toward the external liquidity pool, stopping out internal counter-trend traders.
6. **Microscopic / Low-Visibility Gaps [37:35–38:20]**:
   - *Trigger*: Gaps that are not clearly discernible under standard zoom, or bullish continuations forming below the 50% equilibrium of the range.
   - *Reason*: Weak volume footprint with low statistical follow-through.
7. **Target Already Hit [39:35–39:56]**:
   - *Trigger*: A valid gap/setup appears after price has already tagged the recent swing target (minor high/low).
   - *Reason*: The delivery phase is complete; an immediate exhaustion pullback or reversal is due.
8. **Missing Candle Sweep Post-Mitigation [39:45–39:56]**:
   - *Trigger*: Price mitigates a gap/support zone but turns upward without first sweeping the previous candle's low with a wick.
   - *Reason*: Liquidity beneath the previous candle remains unharvested, leaving the position vulnerable to a secondary flush.
